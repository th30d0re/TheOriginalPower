# Cover Art Prompts — *The Original Power*

**Title:** The Original Power
**Subtitle:** The Physics of Oppression and the Engineering of Control
**Author:** Emmanuel Theodore

Source material for every concept below: the canonical Equation Registry
(`top_20_equations.tex`, E020 / E034–E035 / E061 / E214–E229), the full
239-equation index across 22 chapters (`equation_explorer/data/equations.json`),
and the structural geometry defined in Chapter 1, *System Initialization*
(`Paper/The_Original_Power.tex`).

---

## Shared style DNA

Paste this block ahead of any prompt below to keep the set coherent.

> Technical-monograph cover design. Precision draftsmanship: thin vector linework,
> engineering-blueprint discipline, physics-textbook seriousness. Flat vector or
> subtle risograph texture — no photorealism, no 3D render gloss, no stock-photo
> collage. Generous negative space. Single dominant geometric idea reading clearly
> at thumbnail scale. Palette: deep ink navy or near-black ground, bone/off-white
> linework, one hot accent (signal amber or oxide red) reserved for the extraction
> path. Trim 6×9 inches, 300 DPI, portrait, full-bleed background, clear
> title-safe zone in the upper third.

**Negative prompt (append to all):**

> no human faces, no portraits, no chains, no shackles, no fists, no flags, no
> broken chain imagery, no stock protest photography, no AI-slop glow, no lens
> flare, no gradients on type, no fake math (all symbols must be legible and
> correctly formed), no watermark, no signature, no clutter.

**Type treatment (all concepts):**

> Title `THE ORIGINAL POWER` set in a wide-tracked geometric or grotesque sans,
> uppercase, top third, bone white. Subtitle `THE PHYSICS OF OPPRESSION AND THE
> ENGINEERING OF CONTROL` at roughly one-quarter title size, letterspaced, single
> line or two balanced lines directly beneath. Author `EMMANUEL THEODORE` small,
> bottom center. Type never overlaps the diagram.

## 1. The Square Ceiling (flagship)

**Grounded in:** §1 *Elite Obscuration and the Orthographic Illusion* — the
five-tier pyramid (E, P_uppet, F_enforce, I_buffer, O) whose apex vanishes under
orthographic projection when viewed from inside the base. This is the book's
single most cover-ready idea: the structure is materially real and perceptually
hidden at the same time.

> A five-tier pyramid rendered in thin white wireframe on deep ink navy, drawn
> twice in one composition. On the left half, the pyramid in true isometric
> three-quarter view: five stacked levels, the smallest apex tier glowing signal
> amber, the four tiers below it in plain white line. On the right half, the exact
> same solid collapsed by orthographic projection into a flat white square — the
> apex completely gone, only a featureless ceiling remaining. A single thin sight
> line runs from a small observer marker at the base of the left pyramid straight
> up through the tiers, showing how the upper levels align to eclipse the apex.
> Faint construction lines and vanishing-point marks in the background, as on an
> engineering drawing. Tier labels set in tiny monospace: E, P, F, I, O.

**Variant 1a — subtractive:** Drop the left pyramid entirely. Show only the flat
white square, with a ghosted 8%-opacity pyramid barely visible behind it, and one
amber dot where the hidden apex sits. Maximum negative space; the whole cover is
an absence.

**Variant 1b — the observer's view:** Straight upward first-person perspective
from inside the base looking through four concentric square frames receding
toward a fifth that is solid and closed. Amber leaks around the seam of the
innermost square.

## 2. The Complex Wage

**Grounded in:** E220 `W = ψ_m + jψ_s` and E221 `W + W* = 2ψ_m` — the wage as a
phasor with a real material component and an imaginary psychological component,
the conjugate identity cancelling the imaginary part.

> An Argand-plane phasor diagram as cover art. Clean white axes on near-black
> ground, real axis labeled ψ_m, imaginary axis labeled jψ_s. One bold amber
> vector W drawn up and to the right at roughly 55°, its mirror-image conjugate W*
> drawn in white below the real axis. A thin dashed parallelogram shows the two
> summing, and the resultant lies flat along the real axis with the label 2ψ_m.
> The angle θ is marked with a small arc. Equations set once, small and precise,
> in the lower left: W = ψ_m + jψ_s and W + W* = 2ψ_m. Everything else is empty
> space.

**Variant 2a — analog instrument:** Same phasor, but drawn as the face of a
vintage electrical meter — etched scale marks, a needle at θ, brass-plate texture,
the imaginary axis rendered as a second ghost needle.

## 3. The Reparations Integral

**Grounded in:** E020 / E228 — accumulated extraction as the integral of real
power `Re[V_state · I_O*]` from t₀ to now. The cover idea is a debt that is an
area, not a number.

> A single wide waveform sweeping horizontally across a black cover — an
> oscillating curve, dense and irregular like five centuries of a power trace. The
> entire region between the curve and the baseline is filled solid amber: the
> accumulated area. The left edge is marked t₀ with a small tick, the right edge
> t_now. The fill grows visually heavier toward the right. Above the waveform,
> nothing but empty space for type. The integral ℛ(t₀, t_now) = ∫ Re[V·I*] dτ set
> tiny in the lower right corner, like a plate caption in a physics text.

**Variant 3a — ledger:** Same waveform, but the amber fill is rendered as
thousands of hairline vertical rules, so the area reads as a tally sheet.

**Variant 3b — dual trace:** Two waveforms in phase opposition, one white one
amber, with only the non-cancelling residue filled — the real power that survives
the phase angle.

## 4. The Backlash Constant

**Grounded in:** E034 `Lq̈ + Rq̇ + q/C = V(t)` and E035, the damped RLC solution —
reform as a shock to an underdamped circuit that rings and settles back.

> A damped oscillation curve across the lower two-thirds of the cover: a sharp
> initial spike, then decaying ringing, converging back to a horizontal
> equilibrium line. The exponential decay envelope e^(−αt) is drawn above and
> below as two thin dashed white curves converging like a lens. The oscillation
> itself is amber; the envelope and axis are white on deep navy. At the moment of
> the initial spike, one small vertical arrow labeled V(t). At the far right where
> the curve settles, a small annotation mark. Precise, restrained, plotted rather
> than illustrated.

**Variant 4a — circuit + response:** Split the cover — top half a minimal L-R-C
series circuit schematic in white line, bottom half its damped response curve in
amber, connected by a single vertical tie line.

## 5. The Interference Engine

**Grounded in:** E226 `I_E(t) = Σ ρ_k A_k cos(ωt + φ_k)` (the Spectral Carrier)
and Ch. 16, *The Spectral Carrier: Electoral Cycles and the Interference Engine* —
many issue-axes summed with engineered phase offsets so the composite cancels.

> Six thin sine waves of differing amplitude stacked and overlapping across the
> full width of a black cover, each in a slightly different desaturated hue
> (white, pale blue, pale grey), each phase-shifted from the next. Below a thin
> horizontal rule, their sum: a single flat, nearly dead line in amber — the
> composite that cancels to almost nothing. The visual argument is the contrast
> between the busy stack above and the flat residue below. Tiny monospace labels
> φ₁ through φ₆ at the left edge of each component wave.

**Variant 5a — the counter-phase:** Same stack, but one wave is deliberately
inverted and the composite below is a large, surviving amplitude. The cover of
noise cancellation working in reverse.

## 6. The Parasitic Control Layer

**Grounded in:** the Preface's architectural clarification — the Elite as a
transistor base drawing a trickle of control current while gating the massive
power supply V_cc that the Out-group and Buffer Class actually generate.

> A minimal transistor schematic as the entire cover image, drawn in crisp white
> line on ink black. A thick amber current path enters from the bottom (labeled
> V_cc) and runs vertically through collector to emitter, rendered heavy and
> bright to show enormous power. A single hairline white wire enters the base from
> the side — almost invisibly thin — labeled with a tiny word set in monospace.
> The visual asymmetry between the massive vertical power path and the whisper-thin
> control input is the whole design. Schematic is centered, small relative to the
> canvas, with vast empty space above for the title.

**Variant 6a — self-exciting generator:** Add a feedback loop from the output back
into the base, drawn as a closed amber ring, so the schematic reads as a machine
powered by what it extracts.

## 7. Total Enclosure

**Grounded in:** §1 *The Tri-Modal Enclosure Model* — three obstruction channels
e₁ (communal capacity), e₂ (geographic/economic mobility), e₃ (psychological/
epistemic autonomy), each in [0,1], driven toward S_enc → 1.0.

> Three concentric circular apertures, like camera irises, nested one inside the
> next, drawn in thin white line on deep navy. The outermost is nearly open, the
> middle is half closed, the innermost is closed to a single amber pinpoint. Each
> aperture carries a small tick-scale from 0 to 1 along its rim with a marker
> showing its current value. Labels in tiny monospace: e₁, e₂, e₃. Below, a single
> line: S_enc → 1.0. Symmetrical, centered, cold, precise.

**Variant 7a — orthogonal axes:** Render the three modes as a 3D unit cube with a
point tracking toward the (1,1,1) corner, trajectory shown as an amber dotted path.

## 8. Orthogonal Deflection

**Grounded in:** E219, the Unified Lorentz Extraction Force
`F = QE_mat + Q(v × Σρ_k B_k)`, and the Orthogonal Deflection operator — vertical
class momentum aimed at the apex is stripped of its z-component and projected
into the horizontal plane, where O and I_buffer collide.

> A vector-field diagram on black. A dense field of small white ⊗ symbols
> (field-into-page notation) covering the upper region, labeled B. From the bottom,
> a bold amber arrow labeled v rises vertically. Where it enters the field, it
> bends sharply through 90° and exits horizontally, its original upward trajectory
> continuing only as a faint dashed white ghost line toward a small point at the
> top. Two amber arrows at the horizontal level point toward each other and meet
> in a small collision mark. Right-hand-rule construction axes drawn faintly in
> the corner, as in a physics text.

**Variant 8a — pure kinematics:** Strip the field symbols; show only the bent
trajectory and its dashed unrealized continuation. Quieter, more elegiac.

## 9. The Equation Field (registry-as-texture)

**Grounded in:** the full 239-equation index across 22 chapters, and the
`equation_explorer` visualization that arranges them as one constellation per
chapter in manuscript order.

> All 239 equations of a mathematical framework set in small white typographic
> lines, arranged as 22 distinct clusters on a black field — one cluster per
> chapter, arranged in a slow spiral or a ring reading in chronological order. Most
> lines are set at low opacity so they read as texture, not content. Exactly
> seventeen equations are lifted to full brightness in amber — the canonical ones —
> and are legible. Thin hairlines connect a few of the bright equations to each
> other. The overall shape reads as a galaxy or a circuit board from a distance
> and as mathematics up close.

**Variant 9a — single equation, maximum scale:** Abandon the field. Set one
equation — the Crash Condition, `dM/dt > dΣ_sup/dt` — enormous, filling the
entire cover, cropped at the edges, in bone white on black, with the title
overprinted small in amber. Brutal and confident.

## 10. The Crash Condition

**Grounded in:** E214 (predatory min-max under M_eff < τ), E215, E216, E217 — the
system holds while suppression outruns momentum, and fails at the crossing.

> Two curves plotted against time on a black field. One white curve labeled Σ_sup
> rises steadily. One amber curve labeled M rises with increasing steepness. They
> cross at a point roughly two-thirds along the horizontal axis, and the crossing
> is marked with a single small open circle and a thin vertical rule dropping to
> the axis. A horizontal dashed threshold line labeled τ runs across the plot.
> After the crossing, the amber curve continues off the top edge of the cover;
> the white curve flattens. Restrained, journal-figure precision — the drama is
> entirely in the crossing.

> vertically in wide-tracked bone-white uppercase sans, author name at the foot,
> and a single amber horizontal rule at the head. One small geometric mark — a

**Back cover:**

> Deep ink navy ground matching the front. Upper two-thirds reserved as clean type

## Notes for whoever runs these

- Concepts **1**, **3**, and **6** are the strongest thumbnail performers: one
  shape, one idea, readable at 100px.
- Concept **9a** is the most commercially distinctive if the goal is to look like
  a serious technical monograph rather than a social-science trade book.
- Image models mangle mathematical notation. Generate the diagram and background
  without any equation text, then set the equations and title in vector type
  afterward. Every prompt above is written so the diagram survives without its
  labels.
- Keep the accent color used for exactly one thing across the whole set: the
  extraction path. That consistency is what makes the front, spine, and back read
  as one object.
- The book argues from physics and engineering, not from iconography. Any prompt
  drifting toward conventional protest or civil-rights visual language is off
  target — the whole rhetorical position is that this is a systems document.
