# Five-Tier Cone

An instrument for the 3-D cone (`Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:486`–`:593`), the node-to-circuit-element
mapping (`:604`–`:650`), the Tri-Modal Enclosure score (eq. 1.1, `:713`), and Elite Obscuration: the
Flat Ceiling (`:770`–`:810`) and the angular collapse, eq. 14.5 (`:13751`).

- Published page (private until shared): https://claude.ai/artifact/2JCEYgZqunVHSe9aMkJHfi
- Local copy: open `five_tier_cone.html` in a browser.

## What the page does

- An orthographic, rotatable cone. You can drag it, use the sliders, or use the presets: outside view,
  side view, and the view from inside O.
- The tier discs are slices of the cone and are opaque. From inside O, looking up the axis, the I_buffer
  disc hides P, F, and E: the Flat Ceiling.
- **Tier alignment.** At 1.00 the four upper tiers share one axis (eq. 14.5). Lower values move them
  apart by fixed amounts. The readout gives the share of E's disc that no lower disc covers in the view
  up the axis. E comes into view as alignment falls from 0.5 to 0.3.
- **Enclosure limit.** When the limit is on, the highest viewing elevation is −90° + 180°·(1 − S_enc).
  At total enclosure, the observer is held under the Flat Ceiling. This link is the page's own device.
  The manuscript says that enclosure prevents the observer from stepping outside the cone.
