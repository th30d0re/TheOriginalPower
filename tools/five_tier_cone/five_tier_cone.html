Five-Tier Cone

The hierarchy is a 3-D cone: E at the apex, Puppet, Fenforce, and Ibuffer as descending layers, O enclosed at the base. From inside O, looking up the axis, orthographic projection hides the apex behind the lower layers: the Flat Ceiling. Enclosure keeps the observer from stepping outside to see the whole cone.

What the page assumes

From the manuscript: the five nodes and the 3-D cone, E at the apex on the z-axis and O enclosed at the base (Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:486–:593); the node-to-circuit-element table and the voltage-drop ordering, eq. 0.2 (:604–:650); the Tri-Modal Enclosure score, eq. 1.1 (:713); the Flat Ceiling, Elite Obscuration, and the observer on the axis (:770–:810); the angular-collapse condition θE = θP = θF = θI, eq. 14.5 (:13751).

The page draws the cone in orthographic projection, as the manuscript specifies. Tier heights and radii follow the manuscript's figure: each layer is a slice of the cone, so a lower layer is wider than the ones above it. Two links are the page's own devices: the elevation limit tied to Senc (the manuscript says enclosure prevents the observer from stepping outside the cone; the page turns that into a limit on the viewing angle), and the alignment control (the page shifts the four upper tiers apart, off their common axis, by fixed amounts to show what the θ-collapse prevents). "E visible from inside O" is the share of E's disc that no lower disc covers in the view straight up the axis.
