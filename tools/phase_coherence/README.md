An instrument for phase loading, Φ_load = 1 − |mean e^{iΦ_j}| (eqs. 1.11 and 8.17,
`Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:2517`, `:7193`), and for phase coherence as part of the Snubber Circuit

## What the page does

- 24 subgroup phases on the unit circle, their mean vector (length r = 1 − Φ_load), and a τ ring.
- A run from 1960 to 2030. With "Follow history" on, the identity axes switch on at the manuscript's
  dates: gender 1972, nationality 1974, sexuality 1977, religion 1979 (`:7251`–`:7255`). The ability axis
  uses 1990 (the ADA, from the axis table, `:7240`).
- The Engine as a phase-locked control system (`:14640`–`:14665`): static, or redrawing the offsets at
  each election on a 4-year or a 2-year carrier.
- K, deliberate alignment (Kuramoto coupling), and σ, natural drift.
- The summed solidarity wave against the τ band. The crash condition is the zero-sequence case (`:1233`).

## Calibration

With K = 0, the axis amplitudes are set so that race alone gives Φ_load = 0.078 and race plus the four
1965–1980 axes give 0.403. These are the manuscript's ANES endpoints (`:2522`). The time chart also shows
the activation-period mean, 0.214. After an electoral redraw, the value varies around the calibration,
because each redraw is a new random set of offsets. The τ level (default r_τ = 0.90) is illustrative.
