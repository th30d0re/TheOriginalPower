What the page assumes

From the manuscript: Φload as circular dispersion, the Kuramoto order parameter (eqs. 1.11 and 8.17, Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:2517, :7193); the axis table and activation dates, gender 1972, nationality 1974, sexuality 1977, religion 1979 (:7215–:7256); the ANES calibration, Φload ≈ 0.078 before 1964, ≈ 0.214 during activation, ≈ 0.403 after 1980 (:2522); the Engine as a phase-locked control system on the electoral clock, 24.1:1 identity-band power at the 4-year cycle and 12.8:1 at the 2-year cycle (:14640–:14665); phase coherence as part of the Snubber Circuit (:14668). The ability axis has no date in that list; the page uses the ADA, 1990, from the axis table.

The model: 24 subgroups. Each phase is Φj = θj + Σk akukj, with fixed random draws ukj in [−1, 1] per active axis (opposite signs drive subgroups into antagonism, as the manuscript defines). The base phases follow the Kuramoto equation dθj/dt = ωj + K·r·sin(ψ − Φj), where K is deliberate alignment and σ spreads the natural frequencies ωj. The page sets the axis amplitudes so that, with K = 0, the race axis alone gives Φload = 0.078 and race plus the four 1965–1980 axes give 0.403, the manuscript's two ANES endpoints. With an electoral carrier on, the Engine redraws the offsets ukj at each election: a new set of wedge issues. The τ level is illustrative; at the default rτ = 0.90, the pre-1964 race-only state sits just above it, the kernel-breach condition of the civil-rights era.
