Interference Engine

The Engine keeps class solidarity below τ with two levers. It moves attention out of the class band into identity modes (spectral redistribution, eqs. 8.12–8.16), and it scatters the phases of the subgroups that stay in the class band (phase loading, Φload, eq. 8.17). The manuscript calls these two faces of the same control. The readout joins them into one measure, ρτ, and three earlier instruments measure the Engine from different sides: the phase circle, the runtime-log field, and the shock response.

1 · The Engine's state

Readout: distance to τ

ρτ = M₀ · Pclass · (1 − Φload)². Pclass = (1 − η)(1 − u) is the share of attention still in the class band; (1 − Φload)² is the part of that power the subgroups add up coherently. The crash condition is ρτ ≥ 1.

What the page assumes

From the manuscript: Model C and the control objective with total power held constant (eqs. 8.12–8.16, Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:6914–:6946); the bands (:6955); circular dispersion Φload and its relation to the band-power ratio, "two faces of the same control" (eqs. 8.17 and 8.22, :7195–:7210); the ANES calibration of Φload, 0.078 before 1964 and 0.403 after 1980 (:2522); the Compiled Runtime Log (via tools/interference_state/data/runtime_log.json); the four Laplace fits (:7032–:7035); the Weimar ℛsuppress ≈ 0.03 (:7006); the spectral carrier chapter (:14446–:14670).

The joint measure is the page's construction. The manuscript states the two levers separately. The page joins them with one identity of signal arithmetic: N subgroup carriers with phases Φj sum to an amplitude N·|mean eiΦ| = N·(1 − Φload), so the coherent class power scales as (1 − Φload)². M₀ is the class pressure before the Engine acts, in units of τ. It is not calibrated; the runtime-log panel shows the M₀ that each logged era implies.

Data panels. The page embeds the three processed CSVs from Paper/data/ and the runtime log (built by tools/interference_engine/scripts/build_data.py). It applies the corrected methods of Paper/scripts/spectral_fourier.ipynb (ANES at 1 sample per year; within-series shares; seasonality removal by the mean ISO-week profile) and of Paper/scripts/eq_fourier_electoral_cycle.py (one-sided spectrum with the Nyquist bin; both bracketing bins for an off-grid target).
