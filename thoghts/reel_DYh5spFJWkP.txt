What if the most powerful political move
Black America could make wasn't choosing between two parties
that take our vote for granted,
but building our own Black-led party?
Please share this post with everybody black.
This is the strategy they've been trying
to stop us from pursuing since the first time we tried
in 1972 at the Gary convention.
This is what we'd have to do.
To form a recognized political party in the United States,
you must file for official party status
in each state you intend to operate in.
This involves gathering petition signatures,
submitting articles of organization,
establishing a party name, symbol, and charter,
and registering with the Federal Election Commission.
It's a bureaucratic red tape jumbo meant to deter you,
but I will not be deterred.
I love destroying red tape.
We must hold a national convention
and ratify a black legislative agenda.
A binding platform that every candidate
that we run must uphold.
Delegates from every state, every city, every community
where black people have been underserved,
underfunded, and underrepresented,
which is every state in this stolen America.
Criminal justice, economic investment,
education equity, housing, healthcare, reparations policy.
This agenda is not a wish list.
It is a contract between this party and the people it serves.
No more empty promises from the left
and no more Project 2025 strategies from the right.
Here is where this strategy separates itself
from every failed third party attempt in American history.
We are not.
I repeat, we are not running a candidate for president
because the presidency is a national race
and third parties don't win national races.
Instead, we are laser focused on state legislative seats,
city councils, marrow races, school boards,
and selected U.S. House and Senate districts,
specifically in areas where black Americans
make up a significant share or outright majority
of the population.
And a handful of strategic U.S. House and Senate seats
can become the deciding votes on the federal level.
That's called leverage.
I'm asking you to believe in math.
There are millions of black Americans
who have never voted.
However, I believe a party built by them, for them,
will engage this segment of our population.
I hope y'all picking up what I'm putting down.
The goal is not rebellion.
The goal is representation.
Representation equals power.
The difference now is we will protect what we build
by any means necessary.
I hope to see y'all June 13th
at the Black Unity Cookout here in Chicago.
I love you, black man.
I love you, black woman.
Knowledge is power.
Power to the people.
