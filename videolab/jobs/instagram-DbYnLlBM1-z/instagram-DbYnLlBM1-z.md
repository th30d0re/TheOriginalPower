I would like to understand why, again, you think that men benefit from feminism more? I wouldn't really argue that men in general have benefited from feminism more than women. I would argue that a tiny minority of people who happen to be mostly men have benefited from feminism more than women. And they are capitalists and dark-tetrid men. Technologies like the washing machine, the dishwasher and reliable bath control reduced domestic burden and gave women better control, making careers and education far more accessible.
