# Session Log - 2026-05-05 11:52:55

## What Was Wrong / What Was Requested

Running headers on front-matter pages around Roman numeral xlvi showed **List of Figures** at the top while the reader was in the **Preface** (and similarly, unnumbered `\chapter*` sections do not refresh `fancyhdr` marks after `\listoffigures`).

## How I Fixed It / What I Did

1. Identified the cause: `\listoffigures` sets the LaTeX marks (`\leftmark` / `\rightmark`) used by `fancyhdr`, but `\chapter*{Preface}` does not call the internal mark-updating hook that numbered `\chapter` does.
2. Added explicit mark updates after each unnumbered front-matter chapter in `Paper/Redefining_Racism.tex`:
   - After `\chapter*{Preface}`: `\markboth{Preface}{Preface}`
   - After `\chapter*{Empirical Methodology}`: `\markboth{Empirical Methodology}{Empirical Methodology}`

This aligns the running head with the current section for both even (`\leftmark`) and odd (`\rightmark`) pages given the existing `\fancyhead[LE]{\slshape\leftmark}` and `\fancyhead[RO]{\slshape\rightmark}` setup.

## Challenges Encountered

1. **`\chapter*` and marks.** Starred chapters omit `\chaptermark`, so leftover marks from the previous front-matter listing persist until `\mainmatter` resets flow.
2. **Empirical Methodology.** Without its own `\markboth`, Preface would correctly replace “List of Figures” but the methodology chapter could still carry “Preface” in the header; both were updated for consistency.

## Next Ideas (6 Ideas)

1. Add `\markboth` after any future `\chapter*` introductions (e.g. appendices) if they need distinct running heads.
2. Optionally define a small macro `\unnumberedchapter{TOC title}{running head}{}` to centralize `\chapter*`, `\addcontentsline`, and `\markboth`.
3. Consider `\markboth{\MakeUppercase{Preface}}{\MakeUppercase{Preface}}` if you want uppercase running heads to match default numbered chapter styling.
4. After `\tableofcontents` / `\listoffigures`, if similar bleed occurs into the *next* structural block, reset marks at the boundary of those lists (less common).
5. Re-run **`latexmk` twice** after structural TOC changes so TOC/LOS and marks stay synced in PDF.
6. For `hyperref`, keep `\pdfstringdefDisableCommands` separate from marks; `\markboth` does not affect PDF bookmarks.
