# Review of Cluster B (Iberian) — REJECTED on three of four items

The cluster B report (`gap-ch3-cluster-b-iberian.md`) returned all four items
marked VERIFIED. Three do not survive review. The reviewer opened the artifacts
named in the report.

## Item 12 — Gonçalves, 1441 — ACCEPTED

The only item resting on a real primary source, and it holds. Verified
independently against the Beazley/Prestage translation of Zurara's *Chronicle*
(Project Gutenberg 35738), Chapter XII, whose heading reads "How Antam
Gonçalvez brought back the first Captives":

- « in this year 1441 … the Infant armed a little ship, of the which he made
  captain one Antam Gonçalvez, his chamberlain, and a very young man; and the
  end of that voyage was none other, according to my Lord's commandment, but to
  ship a cargo of the skins and oil of those sea-wolves »
- Gonçalves's own words, as the report quotes them: « O How fair a thing it
  would be if we, who have come to this land for a cargo of such petty
  merchandise, were to meet with the good luck to bring the first captives
  before the face of our Prince. »
- The captives: a lone man taken on the path — « a naked man following a camel,
  with two assegais in his hand » — and then « a black Mooress … who was slave
  of those on the hill ».

The chapter supports exactly what the manuscript needs: the raid was undertaken
on the captain's own initiative, outside his orders, twelve years before Zurara
wrote the justification.

## Item 15 — Toledo Sentencia-Estatuto, 1449 — REJECTED

Sourced to **Spanish Wikipedia**, and the report proposes a Wikipedia BibTeX
entry for the manuscript's bibliography. A 15th-century statute that the author
marked "necessary" cannot rest on an encyclopedia. Re-source to Sicroff,
Nirenberg, or equivalent scholarship.

## Item 11 — Aristotle and Isaac — REJECTED

The report quotes a definition of "proto-racism" and attributes it to Benjamin
Isaac in quotation marks. The artifact it names is the **Bryn Mawr Classical
Review notice of Isaac's book** — a reviewer's rendering, not Isaac's text. The
brief asked explicitly for Isaac's own definition from Isaac's own pages.

This is the same failure the audit has hit three times: a third party's
characterization promoted into quotation marks under the subject's name. It is
what "anarchic" was, and what "New World pioneer" was.

## Item 14 — Valladolid, 1550-1551 — REJECTED

Sourced to **English Wikipedia**. It then proposes citing the passage to
`las_casas_short_account` — Las Casas's *Short Account*, a polemical primary
text of 1552. That work cannot support claims about the junta's session
structure or its lack of a verdict. Citing it for those claims would put a
source behind an assertion it does not make.
