# Firmin 1885 folio verification — cross-check of two independent passes

Two passes were run over the same book without sight of each other's work.
Pass A (`firmin-folio-verification_pass-a.md`) covered the twelve priority
folios. Pass B (`firmin-folio-verification_pass-b.md`) swept all four groups,
twenty-six pages in total. Both rendered every page with
`pdftoppm -r 165 -png` and read the printed folio off the running head; neither
confirmed a quotation from the OCR layer.

## Where the two passes overlap (folios 564, 571, 572, 574, 575, 578-579, 203,
## 205, 206, 209, 212, 213)

No contradiction. Both return VERIFIED on every folio that carries a quotation,
and both report folios 571 and 213 as carrying no quotation to check.

Both independently flagged the same defect on **folio 212**: the mining pass's
wording « cette catégorique doctrine mystérieuse… » is an OCR word-order
scramble. The page reads « l'exposition catégorique de cette doctrine
mystérieuse implantée à l'égal d'un dogme dans l'esprit de nos savants ».
*Catégorique* modifies *exposition*. The folio is right; the wording is wrong.
Two independent readings agreeing on the same correction is the strongest
evidence this exercise produces.

Both independently surfaced the same passage on folios 571-572: Firmin quoting
Herbert Spencer's *Morale évolutionniste* (footnoted on 572 as *Les bases de la
morale évolutionniste*, p. 206) affirming a European "droit d'extermination"
against those who resist invasion. Not currently in the manuscript.

Pass B adds one refinement pass A did not catch: at **folio 575**, « C'est elle
qui dirige la science » takes *la race blanche* as its antecedent, two sentences
up, rather than "[l'Europe]" as the mining pass glossed it.

## Corrections found only by pass B, both confirmed independently

Both were checked by rendering the page and reading the running head directly,
by a third reader who had not run either pass.

- **Folio 233 → 234.** The sentence « mais on a un but arrêté, systématique,
  autour duquel tout gravite d'une façon évidente » sits on folio 234. The
  Claude Bernard gloss ends on 233 and the sentence lands on the next page.
  Cite 234, or 233-234 for the whole passage. CONFIRMED: running head reads
  234, sentence present.
- **Folio 653 → 652.** The phrase « qui n'ont duré à travers tant de siècles
  qu'à l'aide de légendes et de préjugés » sits on folio 652. CONFIRMED:
  running head reads 652, phrase present in the closing paragraph, which ends
  « Non ! mille fois non ! ».

## Caveat carried forward from pass B

The folio numeral on PDF page 224 (folio 203) is clipped at the scan's edge.
Verification there rests on page content — the opening of Chapter VI — plus the
offset, which held on roughly twenty other pages in these two passes. Treat 203
as verified by content rather than by running head.

## Standing

The page-offset convention (printed folio = PDF page − 21) held on every page
either pass rendered. Of the folios the mining pass flagged as OCR-only, two
carried a wrong folio number and one carried scrambled wording. Everything else
held.
