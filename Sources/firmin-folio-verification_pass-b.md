# Firmin 1885 — folio verification report (independent pass)

**Verifier's role.** This pass checks the **[layout-only]** items in
`Sources/firmin-1885-mining-pass.md` — the passages that pass read only in the
scrambled OCR layer and flagged as unverified. I did not write the claims under
review.

**Method.** Every page was rendered from
`Sources/Firmin_De_l_egalite_des_races_humaines_1885.pdf` with
`pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 165 -png` and read as an image. The printed folio was read
off the running head of each rendered page. The offset **printed folio = PDF page
− 21** held on every page checked (Préface pages are cited by PDF page and carry
roman numerals). No French string below comes from the OCR layer; all were read
off the page images.

**Coverage.** All four priority groups were swept in full. Nothing was left
unchecked.

**Headline results.**

- **20 of 25 checked items: VERIFIED** verbatim (wording and folio as claimed).
- **2 items: CORRECTED** —
  1. The "catégorique doctrine mystérieuse" quotation (claimed folio 212) is an
     OCR word-order scramble. The page reads *« l'exposition catégorique de cette
     doctrine mystérieuse »* — "catégorique" modifies *exposition*, not
     *doctrine*. Folio 212 is correct; the wording was wrong.
  2. The "but arrêté, systématique" sentence is on **folio 234, not 233**
     (the Claude Bernard gloss it follows is on 233).
- **1 partial misattribution inside folio 653:** two of its three phrases are on
  653; the third (« n'ont duré à travers tant de siècles qu'à l'aide de légendes
  et de préjugés ») is on **folio 652**.
- **3 listed pages carry no quotation in the mining pass** (folio 571, folio 213,
  Préface PDF 14). Their content is noted below; there was nothing to confirm.
- **1 interpretive gloss flagged:** at folio 575 the pronoun in « C'est elle qui
  dirige la science » refers grammatically to *la race blanche*, not directly to
  "[l'Europe]" as the mining pass glossed it.

## Group 1

### Folio 564 (PDF 585) — VERIFIED

Running head: **564**.

> « Aussi, toute la somme d'ambition et d'égoïsme mesquin qu'il est devenu
> honteux à un homme de concevoir pour lui-même, tend-on à la déverser en faveur
> de sa patrie, ou de sa race pour lesquelles on ne peut jamais être trop
> ambitieux. »

(The mining pass's quotation drops the leading "Aussi," and stops before "pour
lesquelles on ne peut jamais être trop ambitieux"; the quoted core is exact.)

*English:* "Thus, all the sum of ambition and petty egoism that it has become
shameful for a man to conceive for himself, one tends to pour out in favour of
one's fatherland, or of one's race, for which one can never be too ambitious."

*Supports:* §2 motive table — private egoism displaced onto patrie/race.

### Folio 571 (PDF 592) — NO CLAIM TO VERIFY

### Folio 572 (PDF 593) — VERIFIED

Running head: **572**.

> « Pour encourager l'esprit public dans l'acceptation et l'exécution de ces
> entreprises lointaines et chanceuses, n'y a-t-il pas la théorie de l'inégalité
> des races ? N'est-ce pas la destinée des peuples blancs de gouverner le monde
> entier ? Toute l'Europe n'est-elle pas devenue héritière des grandes destinées

(The mining pass's ellipsis covers "et l'exécution"; the rest is exact.)

*English:* "To encourage public opinion in the acceptance and execution of these
distant and hazardous enterprises, is there not the theory of the inequality of
the races? Is it not the destiny of the white peoples to govern the whole
world?"

*Supports:* §2 (572) — the doctrine as the instrument that rallies public opinion
behind colonial ventures.

### Folio 574 (PDF 595) — VERIFIED

Running head: **574**.

> « N'est-ce pas toujours la question de race qui domine en ces élans de
> solidarité, mais qui, édulcorée par le miel du parlementarisme, se change en
> question européenne, en la cause de la civilisation ? »

and, later on the same page:

> « Aussi comprend-on bien que la théorie de l'inégalité des races humaines ait
> facilement trouvé dans un tel état des esprits un ensemble de raisons, un
> appui qui ne se dément jamais ! »

(The mining pass's second ellipsis covers "un ensemble de raisons,"; the rest is
exact.)

*English:* "Is it not always the question of race that dominates these bursts of
solidarity, but which, sweetened by the honey of parliamentarianism, turns into
a European question, into the cause of civilization? … Thus one well understands
that the theory of the inequality of the human races has easily found in such a
state of minds a set of reasons, a support that never fails."

*Supports:* §1 buffer/coalition — the race question relabeled "the cause of
civilization"; §4 item 4 (Chapter XVI anchor).

### Folio 575 (PDF 596) — VERIFIED, with a gloss correction

Running head: **575**.

> « Pourquoi en serait-il autrement ? C'est elle qui dirige la science, cette
> science devenue la plus grande autorité, la moins discutée et la plus
> respectable de celles auxquelles on puisse en appeler. »

The wording is exact. Note on the mining pass's bracketed gloss "[l'Europe]":
the pronoun *elle* refers grammatically to **la race blanche** ("Aussi la race
blanche domine-t-elle partout. Fière d'une situation qu'aucune autre n'a occupée
avant elle… elle doit trouver naturel que toutes les autres acceptent ses
lois…"), two sentences earlier. In the rhetoric of the chapter the equation
white race = Europe is clearly intended, but a quotation should carry "[la race
blanche]" or no gloss.

*English:* "Why should it be otherwise? It is she [the white race] who directs
science, that science become the greatest authority, the least disputed and the
most respectable of those to which one can appeal."

*Supports:* §1 institutional capture — Europe/whiteness directing science as the
unchallenged authority.

Running heads: **578**, **579**. The sentence begins at the foot of 578

*Supports:* §2 (578–579) — justification in place of critique.

### Folio 203 (PDF 224) — VERIFIED

Running head: the folio numeral is partially clipped at the page edge in the
scan itself (only "20…" is visible); the page is the opening of Chapter VI and
the content matches the claim made for 203. Verdict rests on content plus the

> « Peut-être observe-t-on un esprit fait d'égoïsme et d'orgueil, qui a toujours
> porté les peuples civilisés à se croire d'une nature supérieure aux nations
> qui les entourent ; mais on peut affirmer qu'il n'y a jamais eu la moindre
> relation entre ce sentiment, qui est la conséquence d'un patriotisme étroit
> mais hautement respectable, et une idée positive de hiérarchie systématiquement

*English:* "Perhaps one observes a spirit made of egoism and pride, which has
always led civilized peoples to believe themselves of a nature superior to the
nations around them; but one can affirm that there has never been the slightest
relation between that sentiment … and a positive idea of hierarchy
systematically established among the human races."

*Supports:* §3 — the older diffuse prejudice distinguished from the manufactured
system.

### Folio 209 (PDF 230) — VERIFIED

Running head: **209**.

> « L'inégalité des races humaines, si elle était réelle, légitimerait si bien
> l'esclavage que, d'une façon manifeste, le propriétaire de l'esclave ne peut

*English:* "The inequality of the human races, if it were real, would legitimate

overwhelmed by the revulsion of his own conscience."

*Supports:* §2 (209) — the doctrine as conscience-relief for the slave-owner.
