- **Four-way verification:** (1) **MATCH—Black board representation rose:** Table 3, panel A, printed p. 337, reports `Black Director` = 1.544, t = 1.78, significant at 10%, and `% Black Directors` = 0.213, t = 1.66, significant at 10%; the text on p. 338 states that Black-director presence and percentage increase. (2) **MATCH—non-Black-minority board representation fell:** panel A, p. 337, reports `Non-Black Minority Director` = −3.240, t = −3.27, significant at 1%, and `% Non-Black Minority Directors` = −0.485, t = −2.53, significant at 5%; p. 338 describes a reduction. (3) **MATCH—Black executive share was statistically unchanged:** panel B, p. 337, reports `% Black Executives` = 0.228, t = 1.61, with no significance marker; the surrounding text on pp. 339–340 says the positive estimate does not reach conventional significance. The separate `Black Executive` presence indicator is 1.116, t = 1.65, marginally significant at 10%, so the revised sentence specifies the **share**. (4) **MATCH—Black workforce representation declined:** panel C, p. 337, reports `% Black Employees` = −0.105, t = −2.07, significant at 5%; the continuation on p. 338 gives an economic magnitude of −1.4%, and p. 341 expressly describes a significant decline.
