   persist while the material return to men has shrunk. `[operator]` The book defines the buffer-class
   allocation as `W = ψ_m + jψ_s` `[book: Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:606]`, and DC mode (pure `ψ_m`)

   `ψ_s` demand reads as a drift from DC toward AC on the gendered axis. `[claude-inferred, verify]`
   Open question: whether this holds as a derivation or only as an analogy. The book requires the former.

2. **Selective traditionalism and selective autonomy.** The old contract was transactional but reciprocal;
   the amended one keeps the obligations men carry and drops the reciprocity. `[operator]`
   `[draft: The_Gender_Wars.tex §Framework II, §What the Gender War Is Actually Doing]` Already stated.
   The rant adds the sexual-currency version (sex as reward for provision, which centers male satisfaction
   by construction and corrupts reciprocal sex). `[draft: "Reciprocal Sex, Transactional Sex, and the Corrupted Middle"]`
   Already drafted; the rant supplies a first-person case.

   New to the draft as a named mechanism. Candidate framing: an asymmetric channel, where the same signal
   passes in one direction and is attenuated in the other. `[claude-inferred, verify]`

4. **Elite obfuscation by essentialism.** Blame lands on all men; the architects are elite men.
   `[operator]` `[draft: "Men as the Gendered Buffer Class", "Complicity Trap"]` Already stated.

5. **Exception-clause failure.** "Hate all men, partner with the good one" breaks the first time the
   partner errs, because the category is retrieved and the partner absorbs the category's anger.

### B. Evidentiary claims (need handling before use)

6. **Women lie, women endanger men, safety risk on dates runs both ways.** Anecdote only
   `[operator]`. The corpus can show the discourse; it cannot establish prevalence. Pair with
   incidence data (NISVS male victimization, perpetrator sex in male-victim reports) or keep it
   explicitly as a first-person case. The EP04 fact-check found the opposing "yes, all men" statistics
   partly unsupported `[book of record: Architecting_the_operation/notes/kimi/EP04_factcheck_FINDINGS.md]`,
   so symmetric rigor applies to claims from this side too.

7. **Calibration argument.** Generalized threat-bias is not self-serving because it is not calibrated
   to the actual risk distribution; shown by the bi-man comparison and the friend who reserved
   caution for men only. `[operator]` This is the strongest argument in the rant. It turns a
   grievance into a symmetric claim: *miscalibrated threat priors harm their holder in either
   direction.* It also protects the book from the one-sided reading.

8. **Material concessions are shrinking relative to prior generations.** `[operator]` Testable. Candidate
   series: male earnings relative to female, marriage-market sorting, household-labor time use
   (Carlson 2016 is already in `Research/`), educational attainment, hours worked. Needs data
   before it goes in as a claim.

## Register risks (AGENTS.md rhetorical constraint)

The rant is built on antithesis and corrective contrast ("not X, it is Y"). Every claim above needs
re-stating as a direct affirmative before it enters `.tex`. Two further risks:

- "Women want X" generalizations. The book's own position is that the burden is on the elites and the
  contract, not on a sex. State each as a feature of the contract (what it assigns, what it omits).
- The "I don't even like most of y'all" passage is affect. It carries no claim and stays out.
