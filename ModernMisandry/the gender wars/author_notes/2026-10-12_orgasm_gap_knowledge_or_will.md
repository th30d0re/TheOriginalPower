# Open question: do men lack the knowledge or the will? (the orgasm gap)

Provenance tags: `[operator: 2026-10-12]` Emmanuel, in chat; `[claude-inferred, verify]` derived here, not yet checked against sources.
Origin: aside during the reciprocity reel (`Architecting_the_operation/video/reel_reciprocity/`).

## The question `[operator: 2026-10-12]`

> Do men not know how to make women orgasm, or do they not care to know, or are they unwilling? My whole argument is from the male perspective: if you are self-aware enough to see what's going on, why would you do that instead of just getting gratified, when you are already giving non-sexual compensation? Unless you're wired that way and do it regardless, like me.

This splits the orgasm gap into three candidate mechanisms that the reel's argument treats as one.

| Mechanism | What it predicts | How to tell it apart |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge**: men don't know what works | Gap shrinks with partner-specific experience and communication; men overestimate partner orgasm | Look for men's estimates of partner orgasm vs. women's reports; effects of practices (oral, manual, clitoral stimulation) |
| **Will / entitlement**: men know but don't prioritize her pleasure | Gap tracks context (hookup vs. relationship) more than skill; men report caring less about a casual partner's pleasure | Look at stated entitlement to pleasure by context |
| **Transaction**: the reel's thesis; non-sexual payment makes self-centering rational | Gap widens as non-sexual compensation before sex grows, holding commitment constant | No direct test found yet. This is the novel claim and needs a design |

## Field observations `[operator: 2026-10-12]`

- **Feedback is the calibration signal.** He tells partners outright to tell him what works: "If you don't tell me, I cannot calibrate myself." He can often detect faking, and when he can't, communication falls on her. This bears on the knowledge channel: faking corrupts the man's training data.
- **Oral is easier than penetration for him.** Penetration depends on the person and the angle. Harder, not impossible. Compare with Frederick et al. (2018).
- **The porn script runs one way.** Mainstream porn routinely shows fellatio as foreplay and rarely shows cunnilingus. The default sequence is fellatio, then intercourse. If porn is a primary sex education, this is a knowledge channel and a norm channel at once. Worth a content-analysis source.
- **He reverses the script on purpose.** He goes down first to signal that he's serious about reciprocity.
- **Reciprocating first doesn't guarantee reciprocity back.** Sexually selfish partners exist among men and women alike. The practical lesson is selection: find a partner who wants to reciprocate and is willing to.

## Leads to check `[claude-inferred, verify]`

- **Armstrong, England & Fogarty (2012)**, already cited in the reel. Their interviews report that both men and women questioned women's entitlement to pleasure in hookups but endorsed it in relationships. That points to **will/entitlement varying by context**, not only knowledge.
- **Faked orgasm literature** (e.g. Muehlenhard & Shippee 2010, *Journal of Sex Research*). If women fake orgasm often, men get false feedback, a **knowledge** channel that women's behavior feeds.
- **Frederick et al. (2018), *Archives of Sexual Behavior*** (large US sample). Practices that correlate with women's orgasm (oral sex, communication, variety) point to a **knowledge/practice** channel.
- **Men's estimates vs. women's reports** of partner orgasm. A consistent overestimate would mean men often don't know there's a gap at all.
- **Muise et al., sexual communal strength.** "Wired that way" has a measured construct: men high in sexual communal strength are motivated by partner pleasure. That's the self-description at the end of the question.

## Why it matters for the book

The reel argues that a self-aware man in a transactional setup has no incentive to center her pleasure. If the gap is mostly **knowledge**, the fix is information and the transaction argument is secondary. If it's **will under transaction**, the reel's loop is the mechanism. If it's **communal disposition**, the gap is partly a trait difference between men, and reciprocity sorts partners. These are probably all present. The research task is to find their relative weights and whether any study separates them.
