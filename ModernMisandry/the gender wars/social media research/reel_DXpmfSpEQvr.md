Ladies you have been lied to. I mean completely bamboozled by this idea of if he wanted to he would. Now before you tap out give me a moment because there's another layer of this that nobody is talking about and that is if he knew then he could. The things we like, dislike, our needs, our desires, and how we uniquely experience love, safety, and partnership. Instead of doing the work of knowing ourselves deeply enough to clearly communicate many of us sit around waiting for someone else to come around and just figure it out. We wait for them to teach us how we want to be loved and then when they show up genuinely attempting to love us well we're unsatisfied. But the problem is not our dissatisfaction. The problem is that we cannot explain why we are dissatisfied because we've not put in the work to have the language to communicate our needs clearly. I am not talking about teaching someone how to have basic decency, how to be honest, respectful, kind, or just a good human being, how to have fidelity. That is the bare minimum. I am talking about teaching and guiding people through the intricacies of you. The truth is we all don't want the same thing. Women are not a one-size-fits-all and men are not mind readers. That should not be an expectation and the more you grow in relational and emotional maturity you begin to realize how deeply empowering it is to be able to say this is what partnership feels like to me. This is how I feel loved. This is what hurts me. This is how I want you to show up for me. Because once you can communicate that clearly the situation changes. It's no longer if he wanted to he would. Now it becomes he knows and he's either choosing not to show up or he just doesn't have the capacity.
