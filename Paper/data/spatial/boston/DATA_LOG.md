## Gaps and caveats

- Incident dates run from 2015-06-16 20:41:00 to 2026-09-20 03:53:00. The 2015 file begins in June (the new records system began in mid-2015) and the most recent year is year-to-date at the fetch date; both are partial years.
- Incidents with an unparseable date after cleaning: 0. The 2023-and-later source file appends a `+00` suffix to timestamps; the HOUR column matches the naive part, so the suffix is a formatting artifact and all timestamps are treated as local wall time.
- Flagged incident counts per year rise from 170 in 2018 to 810 in 2019 and remain at the higher level through 2025. This discontinuity points to a flagging or recording practice change around 2019; period comparisons across that boundary carry that caveat.
- Coordinates are validated against a generous Boston envelope ({'lat_min': 42.15, 'lat_max': 42.5, 'lon_min': -71.3, 'lon_max': -70.85}); rows failing validation are dropped from maps and counted above.
- HOLC coverage reflects the 1938 survey map. Parts of the present city outside the mapped area are unmapped, which says nothing about their 1938 grade.
- Shooting incidents are incident reports flagged by BPD. Counts measure recorded incidents, which follow reporting and recording practice.
