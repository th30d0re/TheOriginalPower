# FC-01 — Results of the v1 Corpus Pass

**Status:** complete; v1 instrument retired in favour of v2
**Registered:** 2026-08-09 (`505061b`, `fc-01-fictional-corpus-rubric.md`)
**Run:** 2026-08-09 / 2026-08-10
**Codings:** 62 — 31 specimens × 2 independent blind coders

---

## 1. What ran

Two coders from different vendors (OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity) each coded all 31
specimens. A third (Kimi) coded two specimens before its quota was exhausted and contributes
a third rating on those.

**Blinding.** Coders received a packet containing Sections 3–4 of the pre-registration only:
the coding protocol and the instrument. Sections 1, 5, 6 and 7 — framing, registered
predictions, kill conditions, provenance — were withheld. Two further leaks were stripped
before dispatch: the scope-gate paragraph stated its own expected result, and two fields cited
manuscript line numbers a blind coder cannot open, which made E4 uncodable. The three
Concession conditions were inlined as plain text in their place.

No coder had access to the manuscript. No coder was told that any specimen was a control, or
that Dune and Star Trek were selected as a fit case and a null case.

**A pre-registration cannot double as its own coding instrument.** Recorded here as a general
finding: P2 and P7 name their expected answers by specimen. Any coder reading them codes
toward them.

## 2. The central methodological result

Inter-rater agreement splits cleanly by what a field asks.

| Reliable (≥90%) | | Unreliable (≤70%) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 bounded resources | 96.8% | Benefit ordering | **16.1%** |
| A2 positive feedback | 96.8% | E2 outcome | 58.1% |
| F_enforce | 93.5% | I_buffer | 61.3% |
| O | 93.5% | P_uppet, C2, D4 | 64.5% |
| D3 mode | 90.3% | E1 intervention class | 67.7% |

Fields asking **is this structure present** agree. Fields asking **how much, in what order, or
did it change** do not. A3 (87.1%) and E (80.6%) sit between.

The instrument measures presence. It does not yet measure grade.

## 3. Verdicts on the registered predictions

| | Prediction | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Separability: topology holds while `B_k` varies | **Confirmed** |
| P2 | Named negative control fails to exhibit the topology | **Confirmed** |
| P3 | Buffer-less specimens die to one kinetic action | **Untestable** |
| P4 | Kernel transplant more common than termination | **Refuted** |
| P5 | Residue concentrates on substrate monopolies | **Confirmed** |
| P6 | Baseline provisioning pacifies the core, extraction continues | **Confirmed, sharpened** |
| P7 | An `E` barred from recompiling fails without kinetic action | **Refuted** |

**P1.** Among gate-open specimens: Codex 29/29 (100%) across 15 distinct C1 axis types;
Antigravity 24/29 (83%) across 9. Both clear the registered 80% and five-axis thresholds.

**P2.** Both coders independently identify the same two gate failures. Star Trek core codes
four nodes absent and one partial (Codex) or all five absent (Antigravity).

**P3.** Only one specimen in 31 codes `I_buffer = absent`, and it is the control. Every
extraction system in the corpus carries a buffer class; the antecedent is never satisfied.
This also corrects a claim made in analysis before coding began: the Star Wars Galactic
Empire was asserted to lack a buffer class. Both coders code it `I_buffer = full`.

**P4.** Among 19 kinetic interventions: termination 7, interface update 6, transplant 4.
Transplant is the least common of the three. E1 and E2 are both unreliable fields (67.7%,
58.1%), so this verdict rests on a defective measurement and should be re-tested under v2.

**P5.** Three independent coders from three vendors each named the Spacing Guild and the
Bene Gesserit as unmappable in Dune. Antigravity: "a co-Extractor that dictates terms to the
Emperor." Kimi: "structurally more powerful than the nominal apex yet cannot convert that power
into rule." Codex additionally flagged the Butlerian taboo and the mentat complex. The named
residues are monopolies over mobility, reproduction, information and cognition — capacities,
not commodities.

**P6.** Confirmed with a mechanism the prediction did not anticipate. See §4.2.

**P7.** Refuted in inverted form. Both coders code the Apple TV+ Foundation `E2 = interface
update` and the Asimov Foundation `E2 = kernel termination`. The Genetic Dynasty is not a
kernel barred from recompiling; it is a kernel that recompiles perfectly and on schedule, with
the individual Cleon as the replaced interface and Demerzel enforcing the cycle. The Apple TV+
specimen is the corpus's purest instance of the interface-swap thesis of
Ch. `ch:recompile`, not a counterexample to it.

**Kill conditions.** Neither fired. Negative controls did not code `full` across five nodes
(kill 1). Kill 2 is not assessable, because it is defined against benefit ordering, which
measured nothing.

## 4. Substantive findings

### 4.1 Condition 1 of the conjecture is not necessary

| | A1 bounded | A2 feedback | topology (both coders) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Star Trek core | absent | absent | absent |
| Brave New World | absent | strong | full / full / partial / full / full |

Both coders return **identical node codings** for Brave New World. A society whose material
scarcity is engineered away runs the complete five-node topology when the positive-feedback
condition survives; the null appears only when A2 is absent as well.

Conjecture `conj:universality` (`The_Original_Power.tex:15275`) requires all three conditions
jointly. On this evidence condition 2 is load-bearing and condition 1 is not necessary. The
mechanism in the World State is that the feedback runs through the hatchery: the elite
manufactures the caste system itself.

Two specimens discriminate the hypotheses, and both coders agree on both. It is a replicated
finding on a narrow base, not an established law.

### 4.2 The manuscript's prose and its circuit mapping disagree about `I_buffer`

Antigravity, coding the Expanse blind: Earth's Basic Assistance recipients are "a second
oppressed class (O2) bribed into non-participation via bare survival… The model assumes
`I_buffer` actively defends `E`, but Basic recipients are entirely passive."

The disagreement is in the manuscript, not in the coders. The prose describes `I_buffer` as
"recruited to defend the partition" (`The_Original_Power.tex:413`) and as "policing the
partition" (`:476`) — active verbs. The hardware layer assigns it the **dielectric**, whose
stated role is to store "energy in the ideological field without permitting current to reach
`E`" (`:476`) — the one circuit element defined by not conducting. `F_enforce` holds the
current source (`:475`).

Coders reading the prose looked for active partition maintenance; coders reading the allocation
clause looked for passive absorption. Both readings are correct, and `I_buffer`'s 61.3%
agreement is the measured cost of the inconsistency.

Under the circuit mapping the Expanse result inverts: Basic recipients are not an anomaly
requiring a new node, they are the **purest dielectric in the corpus** — allocation received,
nothing conducted. The anomaly is the prose's active language.

The framework already supplies the mechanism that resolves it. Nodes are roles, not persons
(`:423`); slave patrols were "armed white men **conscripted into** `F_enforce`" (`:5359`) under
"legal deputization of the entire white male population" (`:1794`). A buffer-class member who
conducts force toward `O` has been transferred into `F_enforce`, not stayed in `I_buffer`.
An earlier draft of the v2 amendment proposed splitting `I_buffer` into active and passive
nodes; that proposal is withdrawn, because the five existing nodes already carry the
distinction and the split would have added a sixth to hold nothing.

**Recommended manuscript edit:** state the conduction boundary explicitly — buffer-class
"policing" is dielectric behaviour (withholding coalition, absorbing pressure), and any active
conduction of force toward `O` is deputization into `F_enforce`.

Bearing on the manuscript: Appendix `app:universality` proposes universal baseline provisioning
as one of two changes that move a system out of its universality class
(`The_Original_Power.tex:15306`). The Expanse depicts that policy in operation and returns a
pacified imperial core with an untouched extracted periphery. The prescription is incomplete
when applied alone.

### 4.3 The Concession Theorem survives the corpus

Across 31 specimens and both coders, **zero** cases show a non-kinetic reform satisfying all
three Concession conditions. Agreement on E4 is effectively total. Real empires and constructed
societies alike.

### 4.4 Both structural rules earned their place

**Phase-splitting.** Star Wars installation codes `interface update`; Star Wars steady state
codes `kernel termination`. Both coders. Merging the phases would have destroyed the
distinction. Both coders read Palpatine's rise as an interface update on a Republic that was
already an extraction kernel.

**Paired specimens.** The two Foundations, same world and different authorial teams, return
opposite outcomes under both coders — the design isolating authorship from world.

**Within-universe discrimination.** Star Trek core codes gate-closed and topology-absent; Star
Trek frontier codes gate-open (A1 local, A2 strong, A3 active) with a full topology. Both
coders. The instrument discriminates between two regions of one fictional world with the author
held constant, which is stronger evidence of discrimination than any cross-work comparison.

## 5. Withdrawn

Three claims made from the single-coder pass do not survive the second coder and are withdrawn:

1. **Benefit ordering fails corpus-wide.** Codex: 4 holds / 19 violated / 6 indeterminate.
   Antigravity: 25 holds / 3 violated / 1 indeterminate. Agreement 16.1%. Eq. at
   `The_Original_Power.tex:484` is neither confirmed nor refuted; the field cannot test it.
2. **Endogenous `B_k` is the majority case** (C2, 64.5% agreement).
3. **Wage collapse is near-universal** (D4, 64.5% agreement).

## 6. Defect log carried into v2

1. Section E does not specify which termination to code when a specimen depicts several, or
   whether backstory counts.
2. Benefit ordering has no `n/a` code for absent nodes.
3. C1 conflates a socially live axis with a functioning dissipation channel.
4. No field for a deliberately foreclosed axis (Star Trek's genetic-engineering ban).
5. No field for boundary-condition alteration as a response mode (the Kynes programme).
6. D3 has no threshold for "loaded" — 30 of 31 code AC under Codex.
7. D4 has no threshold for what counts as collapse.
8. The gate ORs the three conditions, treating any single `absent` as disqualifying. Brave New
   World shows they are not equivalent. This defect nearly buried the run's best finding.
9. Benefit ordering has no operational definition of `holds`. Codex required commensurable
   measurement across five tiers; Antigravity accepted a qualitative ranking.
10. The instrument does not fix an output format. Coders produced four distinct layouts, all
    requiring separate parsing.
