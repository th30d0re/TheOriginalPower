# School-to-Prison Pipeline Data

## Core Mechanisms

### Zero-Tolerance Policies
- Accelerated in 1990s and post-Columbine (1999)
- Criminalize normal childhood behavior
- Disproportionately target Black and Hispanic schools

### School Resource Officers (SROs)
- Shifted school discipline from educators to law enforcement
- Students arrested for minor infractions previously handled by principals
- Direct pipeline into criminal justice system

### Disparate Discipline
- Black students **3-4 times more likely** to be suspended or expelled than white students for **same behavior**
- Racial disparities worsened in 31 states since 2010

### Special Education Funneling
- Students with disabilities disproportionately routed into criminal justice system through school discipline
- Early tracking: students identified as "problems" in elementary school tracked toward incarceration rather than support

### Funding Architecture
- ~45% of U.S. K-12 education funded through local property taxes
- San Antonio Independent School District v. Rodriguez (1973): Supreme Court upheld this architecture
- Per-pupil spending gaps: up to **3:1 ratio** between wealthy and poor districts within same state
- American Society of Civil Engineers 2021: U.S. school infrastructure grade **D+**, **$85 billion national funding gap** concentrated in high-poverty districts
- Predominantly nonwhite districts receive significantly less capital investment per student

## The Property-Tax → Lead → Prison Feedback Loop

```
1930s Redlining → Depressed Property Values → Low Tax Base Locked In
    → Underfunded Schools → Deteriorating Infrastructure
    → Pre-1986 Plumbing Retained → Lead in School Water
    → Childhood Lead Exposure (Air + Water + School)
    → Cognitive Impairment / Behavioral Problems
    → "Problem" Tracking / Discipline
    → School-to-Prison Pipeline
    → Community Decline → Further Property Value Drop
```

- Same redlined neighborhoods: lowest property tax revenues fund most decrepit schools
- Those buildings contain oldest plumbing leaching lead
- Children already breathing aerosolized lead from highways routed through neighborhoods
- Drinking lead-contaminated water from home pipes
- EPA does NOT regulate lead in school water (not public water systems)
- GAO 2018: only 43% of districts tested; 37% of those found elevated lead
- When schools visibly deteriorate, property values decline further (Cellini, Ferreira & Rothstein)

## Financial Incentives
- Texas: prison/jail spending grew **5× faster** than elementary/secondary education spending over 3 decades
- Massachusetts DOC: per inmate rose **34%** (FY 2011-2016); education aid per student rose only **11%**
- $182 billion/year: total cost of mass incarceration (government + families)
- New York state/local: $1.21 billion in criminal fines and fees as revenue (2018)
- 43 states (+DC) suspend driver's licenses for unpaid court debt

## Carceral Continuity
- 13% of U.S. population, 38% of prison population
- Black men incarcerated at **5-6× rate** of white men
- Lifetime likelihood: **1 in 3 Black men** vs **1 in 17 white men**
- Young Black man more likely to enter prison than attend college, serve in military, or be in labor market (if high school dropout)
- Prison labor: $0.14-$0.63/hour via 13th Amendment exception clause
- Administrative enslavement: prison administration decides labor use, evading constitutional scrutiny
