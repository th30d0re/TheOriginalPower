# Food-to-Healthcare and Commodity-to-Healthcare Pipeline Data

## Food System as Extraction Vector

### Food Deserts and Swamps
- Predominantly Black neighborhoods: limited access to fresh, nutritious food
- High density of fast food, processed food retailers
- "Food swamps": areas with high ratio of unhealthy to healthy food options
- Historical redlining correlates with present food desert geography

### Environmental Contamination of Food Supply
- Heavy metals in soil enter food chain via crop uptake (cotton, vegetables, grains)
- Pesticides and fertilizers contain metals
- Artisan cookware from recycled materials leaches lead, cadmium, aluminum into food
- Imported ceramics often exceed FDA lead/cadmium limits

## Commodity Products as Biological Exposure Vectors

### Tampons → Reproductive Health → Medical Extraction
- All 16 metals in all tampon samples tested (Shearston 2024)
- Lifetime exposure: ~11,000 tampons, highly absorptive vaginal tissue
- No regulatory testing required for menstrual products
- Reproductive/developmental harm → gynecological care → pharmaceutical/insurance profit

### Supplements → Chronic Exposure → Healthcare Dependency
- Supplements largely unregulated for heavy metals
- Contaminated soil sources → lead, arsenic, cadmium in "natural" products
- Consumer pays for supplement → pays again for health consequences

### Cosmetics / Personal Care
- Always pads: emit toxic chemicals including carcinogens and reproductive toxins (Women's Voices for the Earth)
- Phthalates, dioxins, VOCs, fragrance ingredients in menstrual products
- Heavy metals in cosmetics (lead in lipstick historically documented)

## Healthcare System as Extraction Engine

### Insurance Claim Denials
- UnitedHealthcare: ~33% of claims denied (among highest in industry)
- Prior authorization requirements delay/deny necessary care
- Out-of-network surprise billing
- ACA marketplace plans: narrow networks limit provider choice

### Medical Debt and Bankruptcy
- Medical debt: leading cause of personal bankruptcy in U.S.
- ~100 million Americans have medical debt
- Black Americans more likely to have medical debt than white Americans
- Debt collection lawsuits garnish wages, further extracting wealth

### Maternal Mortality Disparities
- Black women: **3× more likely** to die from pregnancy-related causes than white women
- Black women: **41% more likely** to die of breast cancer
- Twice as likely to die from uterine cancer
- More likely to be diagnosed with stomach, liver, pancreatic cancer
- Diagnosed later, expected to live 5 years less

### The Luigi Context (UnitedHealthcare / Class Warfare)
- December 2024: Luigi Mangione alleged shooting of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson
- Context: UHC denies ~1 in 3 claims; prior authorization denials; AI-driven claim denial algorithms
- Public reaction: significant online support/celebration of act
- Reveals depth of public rage at healthcare denial as organized violence
- Thompson had reportedly increased use of AI to deny claims
- Prior authorization denial rates rising across industry
- Cigna: doctors denied claims without reading them (ProPublica 2023)

### Deaths from Lack of Coverage / Denied Care
- Pre-ACA estimate: **45,000 deaths/year** from lack of health insurance
- Rural hospital closures (150+ since 2010): emergency care deserts
- Medicaid work requirements, coverage gaps in non-expansion states
- Delayed diagnosis due to cost → advanced disease → higher mortality

### Pharmaceutical Extraction
- Insulin pricing: Americans pay 5-10× more than other developed nations
- EpiPen price gouging (Mylan)
- Patent evergreening, pay-for-delay agreements
- Medicare forbidden from negotiating drug prices (until Inflation Reduction Act 2022, phased in)
- Opioid crisis: Purdue Pharma/Sackler family profit from addiction → treatment industry profit from recovery
