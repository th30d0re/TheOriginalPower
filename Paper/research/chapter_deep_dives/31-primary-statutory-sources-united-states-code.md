### 3. Technical Vocabulary and "Operands" of the Code
The following glossary defines the technical terminology used as operands within the state’s functional logic:

*   **Condition of peonage (§1581):** A state-change wherein an individual is held or returned to compulsory service based on indebtedness.
*   **Abuse of law or legal process (§1589(c)(1)):** A functional override where administrative, civil, or criminal processes are utilized for a purpose for which the law was not designed to exert pressure on a person.
*   **Serious harm (§1589(c)(2)):** A context-dependent variable defined as any physical or nonphysical harm (psychological, financial, reputational) sufficiently serious to compel a "reasonable person of the same background and in the same circumstances" to perform labor. This acts as a vulnerability patch for the state to target specific demographics.
*   **Color of law (§242):** The execution of power under the appearance of legal authority to deprive individuals of protected immunities.
*   **Specified unlawful activity (§981(a)(1)(C)):** A primary filter for forfeiture logic, referencing a predefined list of offenses in §1956(c)(7).
*   **Serious violent felony (§3559(c)(2)(F)):** High-tier criminal inputs—including murder, extortion, and robbery—that trigger the mandatory life-imprisonment algorithm.
*   **Foreign power vs. Agent of a foreign power (§1801):** The primary gate for surveillance logic. A "Foreign power" includes governments or factions "not substantially composed of United States persons" (§1801(a)). An "Agent" is generally defined as "any person other than a United States person" (§1801(b)(1)) acting on behalf of a foreign power in clandestine or terrorist activities.

### 6. The "Extraction Algorithm" in Practice
The USC provides the logic gates for the systematic extraction of labor, property, and information.

#### Labor Extraction
The architecture distinguishes between prohibited and permitted extraction. While §1589 criminalizes forced labor, §1761 (Prison-made goods) legalizes the transportation of goods "manufactured, produced, or mined, wholly or in part by convicts."
*   **The 80% Deduction Math:** Under §1761(c)(2), while prisoners must receive locality rates, their gross wages are subject to a calculated extraction cap of 80%. This extraction is sub-allocated to: (A) Taxes; (B) Room and board; (C) Family support; and (D) A victim compensation fund (mandated at **5% to 20%** of gross wages).

#### Property Extraction
Under §981(a), the state extracts property through "traceability." The algorithm seizes assets "derived from" or "traceable to" illegal transactions. This extends to "all assets, foreign or domestic" of entities engaged in planning terrorism, specifically seizing property that affords a "source of influence" over such organizations (§981(a)(1)(G)).

#### Information Extraction
The logic of §2511 functions as a sequence of logic gates for surveillance.
*   **Computer Trespasser Gate (§2511(2)(i)):** Allows interception of a trespasser's communications if authorized by the computer owner and relevant to an investigation. This contains a **scope-limiting instruction**: the interception must not acquire communications "other than those transmitted to or from the computer trespasser" (§2511(2)(i)(IV)).
*   **FISA and Consent Gates:** Overrides general prohibitions if surveillance is conducted under the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 or with the "prior consent" of one party (§2511(2)(c)).

#### Freedom Extraction: Penalty Algorithms
*   **Penalty Increase Algorithm (§521(b)):** The system calculates an "increased" sentence of **up to 10 years** for offenses committed within a "criminal street gang" context. This requires three input variables: knowledge of the gang’s offenses, intent to promote the gang, and a prior conviction within 5 years.
*   **Multi-Variable Assessment (§10301(b)):** The "Totality of Circumstances" test for voting rights violations acts as a complex assessment gate where the lack of equal opportunity to participate in the political process establishes a system failure (violation).
