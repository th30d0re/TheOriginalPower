## I. Event Summary

The Weimar Republic (1919–1933) collapsed under the weight of hyperinflation (1923), war reparations, the Great Depression (1929), and political fragmentation. The Nazi regime (1933–1945) redirected accumulated social unrest — originally directed at economic elites and the Versailles system — toward Jews and other marginalized groups through an **engineered obfuscation algorithm**. The Holocaust (1941–1945) is best understood not as an aberration but as an **internal system error**: the German extraction apparatus, having exhausted external colonial targets, turned inward and consumed a population that had previously served as a **buffer class** within the European social order.

## IV. The Algorithmic Pattern: Internal System Error

The Holocaust is not comprehensible as mere "prejudice" or "hatred." It follows a **systemic logic**:

**Phase 1: Extraction Exhaustion (1918–1923)**
- Germany loses colonies (Treaty of Versailles)
- Hyperinflation wipes out middle-class savings
- External extraction mechanisms (colonial empire) are closed
- Internal social unrest threatens the elite class

**Phase 2: Buffer Activation (1923–1933)**
- Antisemitism is **activated as a buffer algorithm** to redirect vertical class threat
- Jews had served as a **middleman minority** — in finance, commerce, professions
- By scapegoating this buffer population, the elite class deflects attention from structural economic failure

**Phase 3: Buffer Consumption (1933–1941)**
- Jews are progressively excluded from economy and society
- Property is confiscated (Aryanization)
- The buffer population is converted from **shield** to **resource**

**Phase 4: System Error / Annihilation (1941–1945)**
- Once the buffer population’s economic utility is exhausted, the system switches to annihilation
- The Wannsee Conference (January 1942) formalizes the "Final Solution"
- Industrial killing apparatus (camps, gas chambers, Einsatzgruppen) operates as a **feedback loop** — the system consumes its own buffer class

**Critical insight**: The Holocaust was an **internal system error** because the German extraction apparatus, trained on colonial populations (Namibia), attempted to apply the same annihilation logic to a population that was **structurally integrated into European society**. Unlike Namibian Herero, European Jews had **no external territory to flee to** and **no colonial power to appeal to**. The system consumed a buffer class that it could not replace — a **fatal recursion**.
