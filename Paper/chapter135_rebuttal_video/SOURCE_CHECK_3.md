## Item 1 — Length of pull and shooter size

(claim: adjustable-length stock lets shorter/smaller shooters fit a rifle; poor fit hurts control and comfort)

**VERIFIED.** The general fit claim — proper length of pull varies by shooter frame and
poor fit costs comfort and control — is directly supported by NRA-published training
content.

Sources:

1. [The Importance of Length of Pull — American Hunter (NRA), 2025](https://www.americanhunter.org/content/the-importance-of-length-of-pull/)
   - Quote (21 words): "A proper length of pull ensures best posture, minimizes felt recoil and I feel it allows a shooter to perform their best."
   - Shows: LOP should vary with shooter frame (12–15 inches); author's 5'2" wife
     shoots a rifle with a shorter 12.5-inch LOP for a smaller frame; wrong LOP hurts
     comfort, shoulder position, and recoil management ("It pounded me to bits").
   - Does not show: it discusses fixed and dedicated stocks, not collapsible AR
     stocks specifically, and it is a hunting context, not a defensive-rifle context.

2. [Length of Pull: A Complete Guide for Fitting Your Rifle to Your Body — Athlon Outdoors](https://athlonoutdoors.com/article/length-of-pull-guide/)
   - Not opened (search-result level only): presents LOP measurement as improving
     comfort and precision; usable as corroboration, weaker than the NRA source.

Provenance tier: trade/instructional press (American Hunter is an official NRA
publication). Artifact opened: full article text via web fetch, 2026-09-25.
