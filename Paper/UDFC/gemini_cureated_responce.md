**2. The True Math of Redlining (Financial Collapse)**
You said redlining didn't stop us, which tells me you don't actually know what redlining mechanically did. People think redlining was just drawing a geofence around a Black neighborhood so people couldn't get good mortgages. No. It was a targeted extraction of institutional wealth.
As I articulate in *The Mathematics of Oppression*, the Home Owners' Loan Corporation (HOLC) intentionally downgraded those neighborhoods to artificially devalue the homes. Why does that matter? Because those homes were the primary assets backing the independent Black banks. By artificially devaluing the collateral, the system made the Black banks mathematically insolvent. It wasn't just housing discrimination; it was the calculated collapse of our entire independent financial infrastructure. They debanked us.

When you funnel millions of cars burning leaded gasoline directly through redlined, captive neighborhoods, you create an environmental catastrophe. We have the data today: the state effectively lobotomized a generation of Black children through severe lead poisoning. Lead toxicity literally limits neurological capacity, impulse control, and executive function. You want to talk about the crime waves of the 70s and 80s and call it a 'choice'? How much 'free will' and 'choice' does a population have when their environment is saturated with a neurotoxin that chemically limits their cognitive capacity?
