# Brief: find primary sources for three omitted claims

**Never run git. Never edit `Paper/The_Original_Power.tex` or `Paper/references.bib`.** Write only
`Paper/logs/kimi_unverified_sources.md`. If you download anything, put it under `Paper/research/kimi_sources/`.

The book's new subsection `subsec:arms_joint_wage` (in `Paper/The_Original_Power.tex`; search for that label) left out
three claims because no primary source was on disk. For each one, find the primary source and report:
- the exact verbatim text;
- a full citation: volume, page, year, act title, and a stable URL to a page image or transcription
  (Hening's *Statutes at Large* is on HathiTrust, Internet Archive and vagenweb; FBI UCR / CDE for the data);
- whether the claim as worded below is **CONFIRMED**, **NEEDS REWORDING** (give the accurate wording), or **NOT FOUND**.

1. **Virginia's 1639 or 1640 act** requiring all persons "except negroes" to be provided with arms and ammunition.
   Give the exact year, act number and wording.
2. **Virginia's 1705 "Act concerning Servants and Slaves"**: the freedom dues owed to male servants, including
   "one well fixed musket or fuzee" (value, conditions, and the section number).
   Also report what the same act or other 1705 acts said about Black, mulatto or Indian people carrying arms.
3. **Rifle share of U.S. homicides.** Use the FBI Expanded Homicide Data (Table 8, murder victims by weapon) for the
   latest year available and for one year around 2019. Give rifles, handguns, "firearms type not stated", total
   murders, and the rifle percentage of total murders and of known-type firearm murders.

Use quotation marks only around text you have read in the source itself. Never reconstruct a quote from secondary
summaries; if only a secondary source is available, say so and name it. Finish with a bibtex entry for each
CONFIRMED source.
