# Sources for the three claims omitted from subsec:arms_joint_wage (2026-10-18)

Kimi downloaded the source files and then hit its weekly usage limit before writing a report. Claude verified each item
against the downloaded files and added the confirmed ones to the book.

1. **CONFIRMED.** Hening vol. 1, p. 226, Grand Assembly of 6 January 1639/40, Act X:
   "ALL persons except negroes to be provided with arms and amunition or be fined at pleasure of the Governor and
   Council." Checked against the page image `research/kimi_sources/hening_vol1/page226_n253.jpg`. Cited as `hening_v1`.
2. **Musket freedom dues: NOT FOUND in a primary text.** The Encyclopedia Virginia transcription of the 1705 Act
   concerning Servants and Slaves has no freedom-dues clause (no "bushels", "musket" or "fuzee"). It may be excerpted,
   and Hening vol. 3 itself was not retrieved: the Internet Archive candidates were vols. V, X and XIII, and vagenweb was
   unreachable. The claim stays out of the book.
   Found instead:
   - **1705 §XXXV:** "no slave go armed with gun, sword, club, staff, or other weapon" without written leave
     (`hening_v3`).
   - **1738 militia act, Hening vol. 5, ch. II §V:** every listed man to be armed "except free mulattos, negros and
     Indians" (OCR of IA `statutesatlarge00colgoog`, `hening_v5`). A margin note is interleaved in the OCR; the quoted
     words are contiguous in the printed clause.
3. **CONFIRMED.** FBI Expanded Homicide Data Table 8, 2021–2025 (2025 edition). For 2025: total murders 13,343;
   rifles 357 (2.68% of all murders, 6.1% of the 5,813 firearm murders of known type); handguns 5,062; knives 1,475;
   hands, fists and feet 623. Earlier years: 2.55–2.61% in the 2019/2020/2023 editions' 2019 figures (Kimi's
   `fbi/compute_output.txt`). Cited as `fbi_ehd_2025`.
