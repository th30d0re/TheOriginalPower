## Verdict

The script is mostly clean of the fixed phrasings, which is why the regex passes it. But it relies heavily on the split-sentence negate-then-affirm move and on paired aphorisms. Nine are pure foils and should be rewritten before recording. The worst are line 214 ("There was no vision"), lines 790 and 799 ("does not ask you to hate… Locate, not hate"), 418, 340, 457 and 685. The etymology states the derivation correctly and gives its limit right after it. Its framing, though, misreports the book. Line 487 attributes to the book a disclaimer ("the title would mean what it means without the root") that contradicts the book's own line 171 and AGENTS.md. It also moves the book's "coincidence" from the household convergence onto the chain. Both are factual fixes, not style fixes, and they should be corrected first. Tone is generally straight. The exceptions are a handful of jokes and "ever built" superlatives that break the straight-face register, plus an over-used weather-versus-circuit contrast that becomes a cadence habit in the second half. None of these is severe on its own, and together they are worth one tightening pass.
