## The organism analogy, and why it flips (Emmanuel, 2026-10-02)

Emmanuel's mapping of society as one organism: Elite = nucleus (holds the code), Puppet Class =
ribosomes/ER (translates instructions into product; the book's "translates Elite preference into
law"), Enforcement Class = cytoskeleton/immune response, Buffer Class = membrane (decides what
counts as inside, defends the line), Out-group = what gets metabolized.

Use it in two steps on air:
1. The organism picture is what the arrangement wants believed. It is one of the oldest elite
   stories: Menenius Agrippa's Fable of the Belly (Livy, ending the plebeian secession: the
   patricians are the belly that "feeds" the limbs); Herbert Spencer's "social organism." It is
   component three, ideological justification: hierarchy as natural and necessary.
2. Run the instrument on it. A healthy organism does not metabolize its own tissue to feed its
   nucleus. When one part feeds on the rest, biology calls it a parasite. The manuscript already
   uses that frame: the Elite as a "parasitic control layer" (fig:parasitic_transistor, tex ~227-241);
   host / host wetware throughout.

Verify before use: the Livy passage (Ab Urbe Condita 2.32) and any Spencer quotation.
