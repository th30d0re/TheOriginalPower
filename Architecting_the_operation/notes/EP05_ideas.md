## Race, crime, and engineered conditions (from EP04 sources pass, 2026-10-04)

Prompt: Nick Powers' stitch, "Black people are not any more likely to commit any sort of crime."
Airable form: arrest data measure enforcement; remaining gaps follow imposed conditions.

- Premise: race is a social construct. African populations carry the most human genetic diversity,
  and Black Americans descend from many African peoples. A shared outcome across a genetically
  diverse cohort points to shared conditions: the common factor is the oppression imposed on them.
- Enforcement: cannabis use roughly equal, arrests ~3.6x (ACLU 2020; book's cannabis notebook).
  Exonerations (National Registry, 2022 report). Stanford Open Policing (Pierson et al. 2020,
  Nature Human Behaviour): veil-of-darkness on stops, plus the outcome test (searches of Black and
  Hispanic drivers on less evidence, lower hit rates). Traffic stops = most common police contact (BJS).
- Conditions stack: redlining and liquidation of Black wealth, bank insolvency, deindustrialization,
  highway routing through Black neighborhoods (white neighborhoods fought routes off), tetraethyl
  lead exhaust (toxicity known since the 1920s; Ethyl Corp stalled the phaseout to 1996), Kennedy
  Krieger lead study, drug prohibition. Lead: Nevin, Reyes 2007, Aizer & Currie; counter Higney et al.
  2022 meta-analysis (publication bias, smaller effect). Present lead as one cause in the stack.
- Comparison: the 1920s crime wave (Italian, Irish, Jewish) ended in absorption and laundering into
  legitimacy (Las Vegas). The 1980s-90s wave ended in mass incarceration. Same conditions, different
  exit. Check the book's Roaring Twenties analysis for the exact claims.
- Culture as downstream: behavior adapts to conditions, then the adaptation is cited as cause
  (Bayesian-prior hijack). Answers the "it's the culture" argument from Black conservatives.
- Intent boundary: the framework claims knowledge plus outcome ("as if intended"). Emmanuel's
  stronger intent claim stays outside the framework and off air unless sourced.
