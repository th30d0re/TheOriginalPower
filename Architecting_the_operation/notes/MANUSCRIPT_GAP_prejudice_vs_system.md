## 2. Internalized racism, distinct from colorism

The manuscript never uses the phrase "internalized racism". Its nearest
material is colorism as the Out-group's "self-executing code" (Chapter 2,
Fractal Execution, Micro/Auto-exec layer).

Emmanuel's distinction:

- **Internalized racism** is a member of $O_{\text{racialized}}$ absorbing the
  system's beliefs about their own group and acting on them, against others in
  the group or against themselves. It is the way a Black person can be racist.
  The harm still lands on the racialized Out-group and the payoff still flows
  to $E$.
- **Colorism** is a separate, narrower pattern: ranking inside the Out-group by
  skin shade, with its own internal buffer tier.

The episode's first draft conflated the two, and it was corrected before
rendering.

Needs: sourcing from the scholarship that defines internalized racism, and a
decision on whether it gets its own row in the fractal isomorphism table or
sits beside the colorism row.

distinction, with a cross-reference in both directions.
