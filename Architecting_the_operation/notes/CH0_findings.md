## 9. Gaps and hazards

This section has substantial hazards; they are not cosmetic.

1. **The closing “proof” is assertion, not proof.** The completed table says five domains use “the same mathematics at a different scale,” and the final sentence declares a microaggression and a militarized police state “the exact same physical force” (lines 1,019–1,034). No scale transformation, shared state variables, derivation, dimensional analysis, or empirical test is supplied to connect the power-grid, circuit, classical, interaction, and quantum levels; placing them in one table does not establish identity (lines 1,022–1,034).

2. **The chapter declares an exact/shared dynamical law before operationalizing its variables.** It says the same equations govern electrons and humans because both systems share constrained-optimization dynamics (lines 393–398, 439–455), yet gives no units, measurement rules, or empirical estimators for $\mathbf Q$, social $V$, $I$, $B$, $L$, $C$, $R$, phase, velocity, or force in this chapter (lines 23–41, 500–580, 663–718). Etymological borrowing and chronological priority do not demonstrate mathematical homology; they demonstrate at most that the vocabulary predates electrical usage (lines 404–437).

3. **The etymological/chronological argument is largely uncited.** The broad claim that physicists borrowed a social-power vocabulary “wholesale,” the Latin derivation, and most dates/examples have no supporting citation here (lines 404–437). Even if every date is correct, temporal precedence cannot establish that a racial partition “obeyed” Maxwell's equations (lines 419–437).

4. **The universality claim is advertised as falsifiable, but its tests are underspecified and elastic.** A failed social analogue merely “weakens” rather than rejects the claim, while any untheorized electromagnetic effect becomes an open research program (lines 469–483). Without preregistered parameter conditions and disconfirming thresholds, correspondences can be added retrospectively.

5. **Several mappings swap component roles or directions.** The five-node table calls $F_{\text{enforce}}$ a current source, then calls it the conducting element/path (lines 194, 239–242)—physically distinct roles. It calls $I_{\text{buffer}}$ a dielectric/insulator (line 195), then an inductor/capacitor in an LC tank (lines 791–794). It says $O$ is sink/ground and all current flows there (line 196), but elsewhere labor current flows from $O$ to $E$ and real power flows into $E$ from $O$ (lines 643–645, 917–935). These are not minor word choices; direction and component identity determine the equations.

6. **The five-node circuit drawing does not obviously implement the prose table.** The diagram places a current source, two resistors, a capacitor, and a voltage source in one loop, labels the current source $F_{\text{enforce}}$, uses the resistor pair for the Puppet potential divider, and labels $O$ as ground (lines 201–225).
