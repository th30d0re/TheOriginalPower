# Channel structure: specials and Architecting the Operation

Decided by the author, 2026-11-06.

## Two kinds of video

**Specials (standalone).** One concept from The Original Power that spans many chapters, told in one video. Not part of any series. They draw on the whole book.

- What Racism Actually Is (YT02v2_ov, channel premiere). Source: whole-book notebook `d3f54f50-61a5-465e-a94a-55d17326c0e2`.
- Planned: how the police actually started (origins of policing).

**Architecting the Operation (series).** The book, chapter by chapter, in book order. It starts after the premiere special.

## Series order

1. Prefaces: A Note on the Title, Author's Preface, Preface, Empirical Methodology (one episode).
2. Ch 0 System Initialization.
3. Ch 1 Dynamical Systems Formulation.
4. Ch 2 Redefining Racism. It comes after the prefaces, Ch 0 and Ch 1 so it does not follow the racism special back to back.
5. Ch 3 onward, one chapter per episode, through Ch 25 Conclusion.

Series episode = chapter + 2 (Prefaces are Ep 1). Notebooks are named "Architecting the Operation — Ep N: … (v1.1.1)". Chapter PDFs, page ranges and notebook ids: `chapters/v1.1.1/episode_map.json`.

The older `ATO_EP01` (Author's Preface), `ATO_EP02` (Preface) and `ATO_EP03` (Redefining Racism) scripts predate this order.

## Titles

Specials are special editions with catchy YouTube titles. The channel opens with one.

- Premiere YouTube title: **What Racism Actually Is (It's Not What You Were Taught)**. On-screen card and spoken outro: "What Racism Actually Is".
