# Channel structure: Architecting the Operation and specials

Decided by the author, 2026-11-06.

## Architecting the Operation (series)

The Original Power made digestible: the 1,158-page book, walked through chapter by chapter.

- **Ep 0 — series premiere: What Racism Actually Is** (YT02v2_ov). A whole-book preview of what racism is. Source: whole-book notebook `d3f54f50-61a5-465e-a94a-55d17326c0e2`.
- Ep 1 Prefaces: A Note on the Title, Author's Preface, Preface, Empirical Methodology (one episode).
- Ep 2 Ch 0 System Initialization.
- Ep 3 Ch 1 Dynamical Systems Formulation.
- Ep 4 Ch 2 Redefining Racism. It comes after the prefaces, Ch 0 and Ch 1 so it does not follow the premiere back to back.
- Ep 5 onward: Ch 3 through Ch 25 Conclusion, one chapter per episode.

Series episode = chapter + 2 from Ch 0. Notebooks are named "Architecting the Operation — Ep N: … (v1.1.1)". Chapter PDFs, page ranges and notebook ids: `chapters/v1.1.1/episode_map.json`.

The older `ATO_EP01` (Author's Preface), `ATO_EP02` (Preface) and `ATO_EP03` (Redefining Racism) scripts predate this order.

## Specials (standalone)

One concept from The Original Power that spans many chapters, told in one video, outside the chapter order. They draw on the whole book.

- Planned, first special: how the police actually started (origins of policing).
- Planned, second special: the origins of gun control.

## Titles

Catchy YouTube titles that emphasise the reveal.

- Ep 0 YouTube title and title card: **What Racism Actually Is (It's Not What You Were Taught)**, under the label "ARCHITECTING THE OPERATION · SERIES PREMIERE".
- Spoken opening: the book runs over eleven hundred pages; the series walks through it chapter by chapter without asking you to read a textbook; this is its premiere.
- Spoken outro and end card: series premiere of Architecting the Operation; next time, the prefaces.
- Title-card visual: the textbook (1,158 pages) slams onto the screen on "pages" and sends shockwaves across the title.
