# Motion pilot notes

- `SequenceAsTheory`: `KitSound` runs in `beats` mode. The causal claim uses `Flicker`; the two causal connectors use `DrawLine`, with the first connector marked as the scene beat.
- `HeartsAudit`: `KitSound` runs in `beats` mode. Each turn header uses `Flicker` and is the single beat for that turn; the definition-router traces use `DrawLine` without beats.
- `TermsInOneBucket`: `KitSound` runs in `beats` mode. The opening question uses `Flicker` and is the scene beat; the bucket seal uses `Flicker` without a beat.
- `PackageAVisuals`: centralizes cue-ms to 30 fps frame conversion and exposes the kit motion adapters, including the reusable `BootPanel` adapter for panel boots.
