# Minutes 53–56 · Turns 244–255

`CoastalResistance` (244–245) contrasts the first direct coastal raids with organized Wolof, Mandinka, and other coastal resistance, ending on the increased cost of kidnapping.

`CommercialProtocol` (246) turns the failed raid picture into a clean exchange circuit: European goods enter through coastal intermediaries and captives from interior conflicts return on a separate red lane.

`ArmsPressure` (247–250) separates advanced metallurgy from the specialized chemistry and boring required for firearms, then shows imported weapons intensifying a regional arms race.

`SovereignPeoples` (251) replaces the compressed racial story with sovereign peoples under external commercial pressure, merchant capital, and a reshaped conflict field.

`PapalTitle` (252–255) presents Dum Diversas and Romanus Pontifex as two date-stamped legal title plates joined by a formal handoff from ecclesiastical authority to monopoly.
