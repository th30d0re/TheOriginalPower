# Stage-kit motion notes

- `TitleOpen`: `Flicker` powers on the title; this title cue is the scene beat.
- `DictionaryEntry`: `Flicker` powers on the definition field; the common-definition cue is the scene beat.
- `Disclosure`: `Flicker` powers on the narration and waveform field; narration is the scene beat.
- `LiteratureStack`: `Flicker` powers on the heading, stack and tripartite field; `DrawLine` traces the two preserved elbow connectors and the stopping rule. The heading is the scene beat.
- `WhyLadder`: `Flicker` powers on the heading, ladder rows, and retained caption. The heading is the scene beat.
- `RoadmapBoot`: `BootIn` scans in the console, `Flicker` powers on each roadmap row, and `DrawLine` traces the roadmap wires. The console boot is the scene beat.

All scene roots use `KitSound mode="beats"`; only the beat named above is marked for sound.
