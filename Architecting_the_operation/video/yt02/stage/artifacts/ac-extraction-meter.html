What the page assumes

From the manuscript: DC mode, P = V·I and W = ψm with θ = 0 (eqs. 0.7, 0.7a); AC mode, S = V·I* = P + jQ with P = ψm and Q = ψs (eqs. 0.8–0.9); the regimes by θ, including Quadrant II for θ > 90° (Paper/The_Original_Power.tex:1150); the Variable Swap as DC → AC migration and the carceral state as a half-wave rectifier (:1240–:1275); systemic work and the Buffer-Class Work Theorem (eqs. 0.13–0.15); the Reparations Integral and its limits, t0 = 1619, tnow = 2026 (eq. 0.16, :1385).

Orientation follows the complex wage plane: +ψm points down (6 o'clock), +ψs points right, and the clock reading is 6 − θ/30. V is the reference phasor; I is drawn at −θ so that S = V·I* sits at +θ.
