Compounding Ledger

Harm compounds; it does not add. Each policy shock multiplies the Out-group's remaining capacity by a retention factor: Ot = Ot−1·(1 − αPt) (eq. 6.5). Four sourced shocks take a normalized 1450 baseline of 1.0 down to 0.019 by the War on Drugs (eq. 6.12).

The compounding chain

Bars show the capacity left after each shock; the grey cap on each bar is the capacity that shock removed. On the log scale, every shock with the same retention factor drops the bar by the same height, whatever came before.

Latent capacity and observed wealth

The chain's 0.019 is latent capacity before transfers, adaptation, public support, informal economies, and partial recovery. The observed ratio is the Federal Reserve SCF 2022 median family wealth: $44.89k Black against $285.01k white, 15.75%. The manuscript models the observed ratio as an output transform of the latent value, not as the same number.
