## Method and limits

Midpoint frames were extracted from the segment clips in `outputs/YT02V2/video/preview/960x540/` with ffmpeg and read with Tesseract. The first 15 words and start time come from the v2 word alignment and episode manifest. Card text was read from the JSON file named by each `Render` field. Figma clip duration ratios use `ffprobe` against the reused YT02 v1 clips and v2 manifest turn durations.

The v2 preview was assembled from SEG00 through SEG15 with the concat demuxer and stream copy. Its duration is 6829.418 seconds, 0.018 seconds above the 6829.4 second target.

Figma authorization remains unavailable. The proposed cue file uses card headlines to identify frames, with `headline` and `item N` node labels as provisional layer names. Confirm layer names and reveal order in Figma before applying these cue values. Manim rendering was attempted. This host reports `no GPU adapter` and has no available Metal adapter. Six current scene rows remain marked NEEDS NEW CARD; they were left on their existing Render values.

`outputs/YT02V2/cut3.log` preserves five lenient-cue fallbacks from the initial render attempt: S02-06, S03-04, S03-08, S03-09, and S03-11. The final preview uses Figma card renders for S03-04, S03-08, and S03-09. S02-06 and S03-11 remain NEEDS NEW CARD. The changed runs in preview2 use card renders; this host did not successfully re-render a changed Manim scene.
